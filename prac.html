Loyola Academy

This is the first ever batch of CSML students at Loyola Academy 😎.


We are the pioneers of this course and we are going to make it big. We inaugurated this course in 2023 and going to end as Golden Jubilee batch in 2026. Tbh we didn't had a good start as we had no seniors to look upto . During any college event there was absolutely zero buzz for ML . No awards or achievements to showcase our skills. But as we all know once a wise man said "The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step." Slowly but steadily students made their mark in different fields such as NSS , Student council , college Dance team and Department level Academics .
